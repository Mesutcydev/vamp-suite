Your Mac.
Within reach.

Remote control with a sense of home. Familiar, precise, quietly personal. A clear glass interface that gives your Mac the room it deserves.

01 · The mark

Two screens. One place.

An open desktop frame makes room for a smaller, connected window. A clear expression of your Mac coming within reach. The native mark, vector masters and app icons share the same Core Graphics geometry.

Daylight

Soft white, deep ink, clear hierarchy.

After hours

Charcoal, chalk and clear glass.

02 · Color

03 · Typography

Native type. A human voice.

Your Macs.

Pick up right where you left off.

SF Pro · Dynamic Type

Hierarchy that reads at a glance

42 pt bold display type, tight tracking. Title 2 semibold for Mac names. System body and subheadline for instructions. Captions for context. Monospace is reserved for fingerprints; codes use monospaced digits.

All text scales with iOS. Long names wrap. Primary actions stay at least 44 points tall.

04 · Materials & behavior

Glass, with a purpose.

Native by default

NavigationStack, Form, Picker, Menu, sheets and system buttons do the everyday work. Clear Liquid Glass on iOS 26 and later; native material on iOS 18–25.

One obvious next step

Choose your Mac, connect, carry on. Setup is a single sheet. Occasional desktop tools live in a menu, leaving the picture and keyboard within easy reach.

Accessibility is part of it

Automatic, light and dark appearance. Dynamic Type and VoiceOver. Solid surfaces under Reduce Transparency. Quiet, direct feedback without a looping background.

05 · Product language

Speak like a person.

“Add your Mac”

Direct, familiar actions. Use Mac, source, and private address where they help. Keep technical details next to the decision they explain.

“Checking…” means checking.

Show real availability. A saved address is not proof a Mac is online. Scanning a QR code fills a form; pairing and approval remain explicit.

06 · Assets

Ready to build with.

Design tokens · Vector symbol · Wordmark · App icon master

The repository also includes verified RGB app icons and Xcode previews. The light icon uses an ink mark on soft white. Dark and tinted editions reverse it to chalk on charcoal. No gradients, colored shadows or decorative gloss. The opening signature draws the desktop frame and brings the smaller window into place in just over one second. Tap to skip. Reduce Motion uses a brief fade; VoiceOver goes directly to the home screen. Keep a clear space equal to one stroke around the symbol. Do not stretch it or add glow. Website and Store product identities remain distinct where required.
