# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Vamp Terminal a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes showing empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling or insulting comments, harassment, publishing another person's private information without permission, and conduct that would reasonably be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to **conduct@mesut.uk**. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, with respect for the reporter's privacy and safety.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
