About the project

Your machines. Your network.

Vamp Sync runs on the Mac you want to reach. Pair it with Vamp Control on another Mac, iPhone, or iPad to access a Mac app window.

Connections stay on a trusted LAN or private Tailscale network. Sync uses signed identities and authenticated WebRTC; every new device needs explicit approval on the Mac. Screen Recording and Accessibility remain under your control in System Settings. Vamp does not require a product account, a hosted relay, or public port forwarding.

Open development

Vamp is released under the Apache 2.0 license. Mesutcydev maintains the source on GitHub, where you can report issues and contribute.
